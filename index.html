    STARMUSICS

    About us

    Music, with intention — a premium discovery experience built with care for listeners who want clarity, craft, and community.

    The developer

    STARMUSICS is designed and engineered as an independent product prototype. The goal is a refined, full-screen music interface — not a generic clone — with glass surfaces, deliberate motion, and honest local functionality until a real backend is connected.

    If you are the product owner, replace this section with your name, photo, bio, and contact links.

    What we build

    • Discovery for artists, albums, genres, and discussions
    • A cinematic player with dock, full-page, and mini modes
    • English, Russian, and Persian localization with RTL
    • Local collections, playlists, and listening history (browser prototype)

    Contact & notes

    STARMUSICS remains a prototype. Without an account, personal data stays in this browser; signed-in profiles, liked tracks, and playlists can sync through the Phase 7 development API. Licensed catalogue audio, shared discussions, and production-grade identity still require additional infrastructure and rights clearance.
